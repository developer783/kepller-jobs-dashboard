Paradox.ai Jobs Scraper

Search Paradox-powered job boards with AI enrichment and export results.

Basic
Must be between 200 and 5,000 (maxJobs).
Search parameters (comma-separated → arrays)
titleSearch – job title keywords.
titleExclusionSearch – job titles to exclude.
locationSearch – job locations (no abbreviations like "US" or "NY").
locationExclusionSearch – locations to exclude.
descriptionSearch – title+description keywords (expensive, combine with other filters).
descriptionExclusionSearch – exclude if title/description contains these.
organizationSearch – organization name keywords.
organizationExclusionSearch – organizations to exclude.
Domain filters
domainFilter – exact company domains to include.
domainExclusionFilter – exact company domains to exclude.
Description & Remote / Agency
descriptionType – leave empty to skip description.
AI filters
Sent as comma-separated list to aiEmploymentTypeFilter.
Warning: this filter is very broad.
LinkedIn filters
liIndustryFilter – comma-separated list.
